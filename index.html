The Opponents

Know Your Enemy. Gooners. This is what they do.

Read this first. Before you play, before you go live, know who you are up against. Gooners are not players. They are the enemy — and they are out to get us.

They never play fair. They wait until the last 10 seconds, then shoot massive points at the last second to hurt the Cougars. They have a lot of money, and they use it to hurt us — not to play, not to give, to hurt. They lurk, they objectify women, they see a body, not a woman. They are really disgusting, and they are out to get us. Know that, and then fight back — together.

The Lurker

Watches for hours and never says a word, never gives a thing. He is not part of the team — he is just taking the show for free, waiting for a moment to spoil it.

The Objectifier

Sees a body, not a woman. Talks about women like things, not people — disgusting, disrespectful, and out to make a woman feel small. Cougars do not accept that — not here, not ever.

The Energy Thief

Shows up to drain the room — rude, needy, always taking attention and giving nothing back. He steals the energy the Cougars bring, and gives nothing in return. The Garden grows anyway, because the Cougars keep giving to each other.

The Sniper

The worst one. Waits, watches, says nothing — then in the last 10 seconds, he shoots massive points at the last second to hurt the Cougars and steal the win. He has the money to do it, and he does it on purpose. That night, they tried it all night — and the Cougars were upfront and classy, and they held the room. That is why you have to hold your biggest gift for the last 10 seconds, and snipe back — together.

The Troll

Comes in to upset people, to pick a fight, to make a woman feel small and drive her out of the room. He loses every time a Cougar answers with confidence and the team answers together.

How Cougars win

Tap together. Gift together. Welcome the new women in. Hold the last 10 seconds together. Say it plainly, say it kindly, say it like you mean it. Cougars are upfront and classy. Gooners play alone, and dirty — and that is why they lose. That is the whole game — and Gooners cannot play it.
